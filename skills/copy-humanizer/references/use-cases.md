# Writing-type use cases

For each type: when it shows up, rough length, and sample lines in English, Malay, and Manglish. Samples show tone only. Never copy a sample into a draft unless the facts match.

## 1. Email

**When:** external outreach, internal updates, cold outreach, follow-ups, apologies.
**Length:** 3 to 8 short sentences for most. One clear ask.

- EN: "Hi Aina, following up on Tuesday's call. Could you send the revised quote by Friday?"
- MS: "Assalamualaikum Encik Rahman, saya ingin memaklumkan bahawa laporan suku tahunan telah disemak. Mohon maklum balas sebelum 30 Oktober."
- Manglish: "Hi Aina, just follow up lah from Tuesday call. Can send the revised quote by Friday? Thanks!"

Formal email in Malay uses baku. Manglish is for casual internal email only.

## 2. Chat, DM, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack

**When:** quick replies, team chat, client chat.
**Length:** one to three lines. No headers or bullets.

- EN: "Running 10 mins late, start without me."
- MS: "Saya lambat sikit, 10 minit. Mula dulu ya."
- Manglish: "Sorry lah, I late 10 min. Start first, can?"

## 3. Social post

**When:** LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok caption, X, Threads.
**Length:** LinkedIn 80 to 200 words. Facebook and Instagram 1 to 4 short lines. X and Threads under 280 characters.

- EN (LinkedIn): "We moved our onboarding from 9 steps to 4. Drop-off fell 18% in the first month. Here is what we cut."
- MS (Facebook): "Kedai kami kini buka hari Sabtu. Datang la, ada promosi untuk pelanggan baharu."
- Manglish (Instagram): "New menu dah masuk, sedap gila. Come try lah!"

Avoid hashtags stacked at the end unless the user already uses them.

## 4. Landing or web page

**When:** hero, feature sections, pricing, about page, service page.
**Length:** headline under 10 words, subhead one sentence, body short blocks.

- EN: "Track every order in one place. Set up in 5 minutes."
- MS (baku): "Urus pesanan anda di satu tempat. Sedia dalam 5 minit."
- Manglish: "All your orders, one place. Set up 5 minutes only."

Web pages in Malay use baku. Manglish is for brands that already speak that way.

## 5. Blog or article

**When:** guides, opinion pieces, case studies, tutorials.
**Length:** set by the topic. Keep paragraphs to 2 to 4 sentences. Use one example per point.

- EN: "Most freelancers underprice their first project. Here is how I fixed mine."
- MS: "Ramai usahawan kecil terlepas peluang kerana tidak tahu cara menulis cadangan yang jelas."
- Manglish: "Many freelancers kena underprice first project. Let me share how I fixed my own."

## 6. Newsletter

**When:** weekly or monthly updates to subscribers.
**Length:** subject under 50 characters, opener in one or two lines, three to five short sections.

- EN: "Subject: Three things we learned this month"
- MS: "Subjek: Tiga perkara yang kami pelajari bulan ini"
- Manglish: "Subject: 3 things we learn this month, worth read lah"

## 7. Ad

**When:** search ads, social ads, banner ads.
**Length:** search headline 30 characters, description 90 characters. Social ads one to two lines plus call to action.

- EN: "Same-day delivery in KL. Order before 2pm."
- MS: "Penghantaran hari yang sama di Lembah Klang. Pesan sebelum 2 petang."
- Manglish: "Same day delivery KL, order before 2pm, quick lah!"

Do not add a discount or claim the user did not give you.

## 8. Product or catalogue description

**When:** e-commerce listings, marketplace listings, menu items.
**Length:** 2 to 4 sentences. Lead with what it is and who it is for.

- EN: "Lightweight 1.2 kg kettle with a 1.7 litre tank. Good for small kitchens."
- MS: "Cerek ringan 1.2 kg dengan kapasiti 1.7 liter. Sesuai untuk dapur kecil."
- Manglish: "Light kettle, 1.2kg, 1.7 litre. Small kitchen also can fit."

## 9. Support reply or review response

**When:** customer support, review replies, complaint handling.
**Length:** two to five sentences. Acknowledge, act, and close.

- EN: "Sorry the order arrived late. I have checked with the courier and your refund is on its way."
- MS: "Maaf atas kelewatan penghantaran. Refund anda telah diproses dan akan masuk dalam 3 hari bekerja."
- Manglish: "Sorry lah order late. Already check, refund dah proceed, 3 working days."

Do not promise a refund or timing the user did not confirm. Flag it instead.

## 10. Proposal or pitch

**When:** client proposals, grant applications, investor or partner pitches.
**Length:** set by the brief. Lead with the problem and the outcome.

- EN: "Your team loses about 6 hours a week on manual reports. This plan cuts that to under one hour."
- MS (baku): "Pasukan anda kehilangan kira-kira 6 jam seminggu untuk laporan manual. Cadangan ini mengurangkannya kepada kurang satu jam."
- Manglish: Not recommended. Use Malay or English for proposals.

## 11. Announcement, changelog, or release note

**When:** product updates, store notices, release notes, office notices.
**Length:** one headline and three to six bullets. Each bullet names what changed and why it matters.

- EN: "New: export invoices as CSV. Fixed: login fails on Safari 17."
- MS: "Baharu: eksport invois dalam format CSV. Dibaiki: log masuk gagal pada Safari 17."
- Manglish: "New: can export invoice as CSV now. Fixed the login bug on Safari 17."

## 12. Script (video, reel, podcast, speech)

**When:** short-form video, YouTube, podcast intros, event speeches.
**Length:** a hook in the first three seconds, then short lines written to be spoken.

- EN: "You are probably saving too little. Here is one change that takes two minutes."
- MS: "Anda mungkin menyimpan terlalu sedikit. Ini satu perubahan yang ambil masa dua minit."
- Manglish: "You mungkin save too little leh. One change only, two minutes can do."

Write for the ear: short sentences, no nested clauses, and a natural place to breathe.

## 13. Bio or about

**When:** social profiles, speaker bios, team pages, freelancer profiles.
**Length:** one to three sentences for short bios. Up to 120 words for longer ones.

- EN: "I help small clinics write clearer patient emails. Based in Shah Alam."
- MS: "Saya membantu klinik kecil menulis emel pesakit yang lebih jelas. Berpangkalan di Shah Alam."
- Manglish: "I help small clinic write clearer patient email lah. Based in Shah Alam."

## 14. Invitation or RSVP

**When:** weddings, events, meetups, open houses, team invites.
**Length:** date, time, place, and one line on why. Add RSVP deadline if given.

- EN: "Join us for the launch on 14 November, 7pm at Menara KL. Please RSVP by 7 November."
- MS (formal): "Dengan hormatnya dijemput ke majlis pelancaran pada 14 November, jam 7 malam, di Menara KL. Sila RSVP sebelum 7 November."
- MS (santai): "Jom datang launch kami 14 November, 7 malam, Menara KL. RSVP before 7 November ya."
- Manglish: "Come join our launch, 14 Nov, 7pm, Menara KL. RSVP by 7 Nov, don't forget ah!"

Wedding and religious invitations keep baku or the family's usual register.

## 15. Internal memo

**When:** policy changes, team announcements, meeting notes, status updates.
**Length:** lead with the decision or ask. Then the reason, then the next step.

- EN: "From 1 December, leave requests go through the new portal. Please submit by the 25th of each month."
- MS: "Mulai 1 Disember, permohonan cuti akan dibuat melalui portal baharu. Sila hantar sebelum 25 setiap bulan."
- Manglish: "From 1 Dec, leave request must use the new portal. Submit before the 25th each month, ok?"

## 16. Job post or application message

**When:** job ads, recruiter messages, cover letters, LinkedIn outreach for jobs.
**Length:** job post 150 to 300 words. Application message 3 to 5 sentences.

- EN: "We are hiring a customer support lead to run a team of four. You will own response times and weekly QA."
- MS: "Kami mencari Ketua Sokongan Pelanggan untuk mengetuai pasukan empat orang. Anda akan bertanggungjawab ke atas masa respons dan kualiti kerja mingguan."
- Manglish: Not recommended for job posts. Use Malay or English for applications.

---

## Out of scope

- Legal clauses, contracts, and terms of service: keep the user's wording and flag for a qualified reviewer.
- Academic writing and citations: keep the user's references exactly.
- Product UI strings and full UX audits: use the `ux-writer` skill.
